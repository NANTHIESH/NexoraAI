# Drug and Alcohol Policy

Our priority is a safe, comfortable and productive environment for everyone we work with. Drugs and alcohol can contribute to a culture of harassment or one that feels unsafe for some employees, so this policy is meant to prevent harassment and make work at Nexora feel safe.

**Every employee is responsible for making sure their own consumption does not make anyone else feel uncomfortable or unsafe.** Employees who violate this policy may face disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

- Beer or wine may occasionally be brought into the office to celebrate an achievement, a birthday or another milestone. Events at the office are alcohol-free unless it was agreed otherwise in advance.
- Illegal drugs must not be used, bought, sold or distributed at work, in the office or outside the office while representing Nexora.
- Nobody may drive or operate equipment on company business while impaired.
