# Employee Privacy and Email Use

## Workspace Privacy

Nexora tries to monitor employees as little as possible and wants everyone to feel at home in their workspace. Company property such as desks, laptops and other devices may still be inspected when needed to ensure safety and compliance with company policies, always in line with applicable law.

## Company Email and Internet

Company email and internet access are company resources. Messages sent from a Nexora address belong to the company. Company systems are not actively monitored, but the company may review usage when necessary to protect people, data and company property and where the law allows it. Do not expect privacy for messages you send through company systems.

## Personal Use

The email system is meant for company business. Occasional personal use is acceptable as long as it does not interfere with your work.

## Professional Tone

When you write from a Nexora email address or social account, you represent Nexora. Keep messages professional. Email and chat can be saved, printed and forwarded, so do not write anything you would not want the team, a client or a competitor to read. All conduct rules also apply to email: no harassing, discriminatory or threatening messages, and no disclosure of trade secrets.

## Email Security

- Do not open attachments from senders you do not recognise, or attachments with strange names that are not mentioned in the message.
- Report suspected viruses or phishing to the DevOps Engineer immediately.
- Never share your email password with anyone, including colleagues or family members.
- Do not forward company email or attachments to personal email accounts.

## Internet Use

Nexora may monitor internet use on company devices where the law allows it. Your internet use on company devices is therefore not private.
