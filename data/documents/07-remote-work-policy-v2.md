# Remote Work Policy

This version replaces version 1.

## Remote Days

- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- The remaining days are worked at the office or at the client site when required.
- Remote days are agreed with your manager at the start of each week so that the team knows who is where.

## Core Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. During core hours you are reachable in chat and join scheduled meetings, whether you work from home or from the office.

## Remote Setup

- Work from a quiet place with a reliable connection and use the company VPN for internal systems.
- Company equipment stays in the country you live in. Working from another country needs approval from the Operations Manager first because of tax and insurance rules.
- Follow the Information Security document at home as well: lock your screen and keep client data on the company drive.
