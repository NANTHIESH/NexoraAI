# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct applies to all Nexora team members, contractors and visitors in the Nexora office, in Nexora online spaces (chat, video calls, social media accounts) and at all events hosted by Nexora.

## Inclusive Environment

Nexora is dedicated to an inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, colour, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability (physical or mental), genetic information, parental status, marital status, political affiliation, socioeconomic background, neurodiversity, physical appearance, body size or computing experience. Calling attention to differences can feel alienating.

## Harassment

We do not tolerate harassment in any form. Harassment includes offensive verbal comments related to the characteristics above, sexual images in public spaces, deliberate intimidation, stalking, following, photography or recording without consent, sustained disruption of talks or events, inappropriate physical contact and unwelcome sexual attention. Harassment does not need to be recognised as unwanted by anyone other than the person being harassed.

Sexist, racist and other exclusionary jokes can be offensive to those around you. They are not appropriate and will not be tolerated.

## What to Do

Participants asked to stop any harassing behaviour must comply immediately. People who violate these rules may be sanctioned or removed from company spaces and events. If you are being harassed, see that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concern, contact the Owner or the Operations Manager immediately.
