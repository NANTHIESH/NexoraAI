# Other Protected Absences

## Pregnancy-Related Medical Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of pregnancy, childbirth or a related medical condition, including prenatal care, are protected by law. The length of leave follows the applicable law. Intermittent leave or a reduced work schedule may be agreed. Please tell your manager or the Operations Manager as early as you can.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees who need time off after the death of an immediate family member should tell their manager. Paid bereavement leave is granted for up to 5 working days for the death of a spouse, partner, child, stepchild, grandchild, parent, stepparent, parent-in-law, grandparent or sibling.

## Jury Duty or Witness Summons

Employees receive paid time to serve on a jury or as a witness, for the time the summons requires. Give a copy of the summons to your manager as soon as reasonably possible. If the duty takes less than four hours per day, you may be asked to work remotely for the rest of the day.

## Other Leaves of Absence

Nexora grants other leaves of absence as required by law. Unless the law says otherwise, these leaves are unpaid.
