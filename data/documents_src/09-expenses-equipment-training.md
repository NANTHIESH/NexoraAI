# Expenses, Equipment and Training Budget

## Expenses

- Expenses above 100 EUR need owner approval before you spend the money.
- Expenses of 100 EUR or less can be approved by your manager.
- Submit receipts within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses without a receipt are not reimbursed.
- Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next payroll run.

## Equipment

- Every employee gets a company laptop and the accessories needed for the job. Laptops are renewed after about three years.
- Equipment requests go to the Operations Manager. Equipment stays company property and is returned when you leave.

## Training Budget

- The training budget is 1,000 EUR per developer per year.
- Courses, books and conference tickets can be paid from the budget. Agree the choice with the Engineering Lead first.
- Time spent at a conference or course agreed in advance counts as working time.
- Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
