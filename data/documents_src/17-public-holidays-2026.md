# Public Holidays 2026

Nexora observes the following public holidays in 2026. Public holidays do not count as vacation days.

## Holiday List

- New Year's Day: Thursday, 1 January
- Good Friday: Friday, 3 April
- Easter Monday: Monday, 6 April
- Labour Day: Friday, 1 May
- Ascension Day: Thursday, 14 May
- Whit Monday: Monday, 25 May
- Day of German Unity: Saturday, 3 October
- Christmas Day: Friday, 25 December
- Boxing Day: Saturday, 26 December

## Company Closing Days

The office is closed on Christmas Eve (Thursday, 24 December) and New Year's Eve (Thursday, 31 December). These two days are paid and do not use vacation days.

## Holidays on Weekends

If a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, no replacement day is given.
