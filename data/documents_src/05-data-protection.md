# Data Protection, Retention, NDA and IP

## Handling Customer Data

- Customer data must never be sent to personal email or stored on personal devices. Use the company drive only.
- Customer data is shared inside the company only with people who need it for their work.
- Test environments use anonymised or synthetic data unless the client agrees otherwise in writing.

## GDPR

- Nexora processes personal data only for the purpose agreed with the client or employee.
- Requests from people about their own data are forwarded to the Operations Manager on the same working day and answered within one month.
- A suspected personal data breach is reported to the Owner immediately. Authorities are informed within 72 hours where required.

## Data Retention

- Project data is deleted or returned to the client 12 months after the contract ends, unless the contract says otherwise.
- Invoices and accounting records are kept for the period required by commercial and tax law.
- Chat messages older than 2 years are removed automatically.

## NDA and Intellectual Property

- Every employee and contractor signs a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before starting work.
- Work created for a client belongs to the client as set out in the contract. Work created for Nexora belongs to Nexora.
- Client code and documents must not be used in personal projects or published without written permission.
