# Payroll and Salary Payments

## Payment Date

- Salaries are paid on the 28th of each month. If the 28th is not a working day, salaries are paid on the last working day before it.
- Payslips are sent on the 28th of each month through the payroll portal.

## Changes and Corrections

- Changes to bank details or tax information must be sent to the Operations Manager by the 20th of the month so they apply to that month's payroll.
- If you think your payslip is wrong, contact the Operations Manager within 30 days of receiving it.

## Related Payments

- Approved expense claims are paid together with the next payroll run.
- Unpaid leave and long-term absences are reflected in the payroll run of the following month.
