# Client Onboarding, Invoicing and Support SLA

## Client Onboarding

1. The Sales Manager hands over the signed contract to the Project Manager.
2. The Project Manager runs a kick-off meeting, agrees the scope and names the client contact.
3. Access to project tools is set up for the client contact and the NDA is confirmed.

## Invoicing

- Retainers are invoiced on the 1st of each month. Milestones are invoiced when the client accepts them.
- The payment term is 30 days.
- The Operations Manager sends a reminder when an invoice is 7 days overdue.

## Support Hours and Response Times

- Support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00.
- Priority A (service down): first response within 1 hour during support hours.
- Priority B (feature broken): first response within 4 hours.
- Priority C (question or minor issue): first response within 2 working days.
