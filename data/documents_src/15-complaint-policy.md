# Complaint Policy

Nexora is committed to a safe work environment that is free of threats to the health, safety and wellbeing of the people who work here. This includes harassment, discrimination, violations of health and safety rules and violence.

## Open-Door Policy

Employees are encouraged to report work-related concerns. If something about your job is bothering you, discuss it with your manager as soon as possible. If you do not feel comfortable doing that, raise the issue with the Owner or the Operations Manager.

## How Complaints Are Handled

- Any employee who witnesses or is subject to inappropriate conduct can report it to the Owner or the Operations Manager. We encourage you to come forward even if the issue is not explicitly covered by a written policy.
- After a complaint is made, the Owner and the Operations Manager decide how to handle it. If one of them is the subject of the complaint, the other decides.
- Complaints about harassment or gross misconduct by the Owner or the Operations Manager lead to an outside investigator.
- With the employee's permission, the company conducts a complete and impartial investigation. All complaints are handled as confidentially as possible.
- When the investigation is complete, the company takes corrective action if appropriate.

## No Retaliation

Nexora does not allow retaliation against any employee who makes a complaint in good faith or takes part in an investigation. If you believe you are being treated negatively because of a complaint, report it immediately to the Owner or the Operations Manager.
