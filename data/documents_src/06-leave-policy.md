# Leave Policy: Vacation and Sick Leave

## Vacation

- Full-time employees get 25 days of paid vacation per year. Part-time employees get a proportional number of days.
- Vacation must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance in the leave tool. Managers answer requests within 3 working days.
- Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March.
- Public holidays do not count as vacation days.
- Two team members of the same project should not be away at the same time without agreement from the Project Manager.

## Sick Leave

- Notify your manager before 09:30 on day one of sick leave by phone or chat.
- A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days of sick leave and must be given to the Operations Manager.
- Sick days do not reduce vacation days.
- Please do not work while you are sick. If you are well enough to work from home, agree this with your manager first.
