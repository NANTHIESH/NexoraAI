# Remote Work Policy

This version has been replaced by version 2 and is kept for reference only.

## Remote Days

- Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
- Remote days are agreed with your manager.

## Core Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. During core hours you are reachable in chat and join scheduled meetings.

## Remote Setup

- Use the company VPN for internal systems.
- Keep client data on the company drive.
