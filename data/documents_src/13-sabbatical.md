# Sabbatical

After every 5 years of working at Nexora, employees are encouraged to take a one-quarter (12 contiguous weeks) paid sabbatical.

## Purpose

During the sabbatical, employees work on a passion project outside the scope of their normal work. This could be researching a topic of interest, helping a non-profit organisation, or something else that excites them.

The sabbatical is a time of self-improvement and exploration with a central activity or theme. At the end, the employee presents what they did to the team. It helps the employee learn about another area of interest and avoid burnout from working on one thing for too long.

## Conditions

- To keep the sabbatical from being disruptive to the team, employees should have taken fewer than twelve weeks of paid time off in the year before the sabbatical.
- The team must be told at least 4 weeks in advance.
- Employees are expected to return to work at Nexora after the sabbatical and not use it as a soft exit from the company.
- Paid time off of any kind, including a sabbatical, does not accrue additional paid time off in the form of vacation days, sick days or time towards the next sabbatical.
