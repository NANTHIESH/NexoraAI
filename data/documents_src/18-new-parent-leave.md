# New Parent Leave

Nexora offers 12 weeks of paid leave for all full-time employees, regardless of gender or sexual identity, after the birth or adoption of a child. The time is for the new parent to welcome the child into their home and family. The leave should be taken within a year after the birth or adoption.

## Notice

Employees should give the team as much notice as possible before they take new parent leave. There is no fixed notice period, because parenthood can be unexpected and sensitive, but the more the team can anticipate the absence, the easier it is to handle.

## Paid Time Off

Paid time off of any kind, including new parent leave, does not accrue additional paid time off in the form of vacation days, sick days or time towards a sabbatical. For example, an employee who has worked at Nexora for 4 years is eligible for a sabbatical after 1 more year of work. If they take 12 weeks of new parent leave, they still need 1 full year of work after returning before they are eligible.

## Statutory Payments

Statutory parental benefits that apply in your country are paid in the usual way. Nexora tops them up so that the employee keeps their regular salary, and does not pay the full salary on top of the statutory benefit.
